DJI Agras is the agricultural drone family built for spraying and spreading across farms, with tanks ranging from 20 litres on the T25 to larger capacities on the T50 and T100, plus radar and terrain following for precise, even coverage. These are working aircraft for serious farming, not hobby drones. Ask on WhatsApp which Agras model is in stock, as this page covers the series rather than a single model.

Highlights

  • Big drone, big jobs
  • Tanks from 20 L to 100 L
  • Radar, terrain follow, wide swaths
  • DJI's smart agricultural equipment — spraying and spreading drones
  • Across the range, spray tanks run from 20 L on the T25 to 40 L on the T50
  • Phased-array radar and vision sensing with terrain following on slopes up to 50°
